Merry Christmas!

With love for Grammy & Granddaddy

For Grammy

Dear Grammy, whose heart overflows with such grace, A warmth like the sun lights your dear, loving face. You taught us that legacies bloom from the heart, That living with purpose is life's truest art. From Wellesley's halls to the lives that you've changed, Your wisdom and kindness have never been strange. A pioneer blazing trails bright and new, Helping others find paths that are honest and true. Your flute fills the air with such sweet melody, A song of devotion to your whole family. You've shown us that age is just time to grow wise, To cherish each moment, each laugh, every sunrise. This Christmas we celebrate all that you are: Our Grammy, our guide, our own Northern Star. With love overflowing, we thank you today For showing us life in your own special way.

For Granddaddy

Dear Granddaddy, scholar of health and of care, A mind so distinguished, a heart beyond rare. From Harvard's proud halls to the RAND days of old, You've written the chapters where great truths are told. A Fulbright young scholar who ventured afar, Then rose to become an illustrious star. For thirty long years, you shaped how we know The economics of health and how systems can grow. Distinguished and honored, yet humble you stay, Still teaching, still learning, still finding your way. MacArthur Professor, a title well-earned, For the millions of lives your research has turned. But greater than papers and prizes so grand, Is the way that you've held your dear family's hand. This Christmas we honor the man that you are: Our Granddaddy, our hero, our own guiding star. With love and with gratitude, now and always, We cherish these moments, these warm holiday days.

Made with love for the best grandparents in the world
